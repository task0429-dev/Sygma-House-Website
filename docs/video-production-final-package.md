## 60-Second Screenplay

0-8 seconds:

The door opens. A Somali caregiver greets an adult daughter with calm warmth. The daughter pauses at the threshold, clearly carrying the weight of the decision. The caregiver steps aside and welcomes her in.

Female VO:

> Finding care for someone you love is personal.

8-22 seconds:

The daughter walks through the living room. She notices the quiet home rhythm, not a facility feeling. A resident sits near the window choosing a comfortable seat. A caregiver speaks nearby, relaxed and attentive.

Male VO:

> You want safety. You want dignity. And you want to know they will still feel at home.

22-45 seconds:

Life inside Sygma House: tea placed nearby, meal prep, clean room detail, medication organization, a resident walking with support available but not forced, conversation at the dining table, a family member asking questions.

Female VO:

> At Sygma House, daily support happens in a real home setting, with personal attention, calm routines, and care that respects each resident as a whole person.

45-55 seconds:

The daughter sees the resident smile during a natural conversation. Her expression shifts from concern to relief.

Male VO:

> Small-home assisted living in Minnesota, built around comfort, trust, and family peace of mind.

55-60 seconds:

Exterior golden hour shot. End card with logo, website, and CTA.

Female VO:

> Visit SygmaHouse.com to schedule a tour.

## AI Video Pipeline Prompt Guardrails

Do:

- Use realistic assisted living home interiors.
- Use natural Minnesota daylight.
- Show small imperfections: real hand movement, real pauses, natural smiles.
- Keep Somali caregiver representation organic.
- Show residents making choices and moving with dignity.

Do not:

- Use fake AI avatars.
- Over-glamorize care.
- Show helplessness.
- Use hospital visuals.
- Use synthetic glossy healthcare stock footage.
- Make diversity an announced message.
