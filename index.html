About Sygma House

A licensed residential assisted living home serving adults 18+ in Minnesota. Currently accepting residents.

Our Story

Sygma House was founded with a single purpose: to create the kind of assisted living home we would want for our own family members. Not a clinical facility, not a large institution — a genuine home where people are known by name, not room number.

We serve adults 18 and older who need daily support but want to maintain their independence and dignity in a residential setting. With only five residents, our staff have the time and capacity to truly care for each individual.

Our home is licensed by the State of Minnesota under Chapter 144G (the Uniform Demonstrated Ability and Licensure Standards for Assisted Living Act) and we welcome residents on private pay and Medicaid waiver programs including the Elderly Waiver.

Comprehensive care and support services tailored to each resident's individual needs and care plan.

Personal Care

Bathing, grooming, dressing, hygiene, and continence care provided with privacy and respect. Staff are trained in person-centered approaches.

Medication Management

Medication assistance and administration by trained staff. Coordination with pharmacies, prescribers, and medication reviews.

Meals & Nutrition

Three home-cooked meals and snacks daily. Dietary accommodations for medical needs, cultural preferences, and personal tastes.

Mobility Assistance

Transfer assistance, ambulation support, and coordination with physical therapy. We support all mobility levels including wheelchair users.

Memory care-informed approaches for residents with dementia or cognitive impairment. Structure, familiar routine, and a calm environment.

Health Coordination

Communication with physicians, home health agencies, hospice providers, and specialists. Transportation assistance for medical appointments.

Full housekeeping, linen changes, and personal laundry included. A clean, comfortable home maintained to high standards.

Elderly Waiver (EW)
The MN Elderly Waiver allows seniors 65+ who meet nursing home level of care to receive services in the community, including residential settings like Sygma House. Administered through county human services.
Waiver

MnCHOICES Assessment
MnCHOICES is the assessment tool used in Minnesota to determine waiver eligibility and level of care. Conducted by county social workers or managed care organizations. Contact your county to initiate.

Medical Assistance (Medicaid)

Chapter 144G Overview

Minnesota Chapter 144G (UDALSA) established uniform standards for all assisted living facilities in the state. Requirements include staff training, resident rights protections, disclosure obligations, and licensing standards enforced by MDH.
