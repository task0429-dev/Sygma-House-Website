Admissions & Intake

Everything you need to know about the admission process at Sygma House.

Good Fit

Who May Be a Good Fit

  • ✓ Adults who need daily living assistance but do not require skilled nursing or 24-hour medical oversight
  • ✓ Individuals who benefit from a structured, supervised home environment
  • ✓ Residents who are medically stable and whose care needs can be met within our service capabilities
  • ✓ Those who prefer a small, homelike setting over a large care facility
  • ✓ Private pay or Medicaid waiver eligible individuals
  • ✓ Individuals transitioning from hospital, rehab, or another care setting
May Not Be a Fit

Who May Not Be a Good Fit

  • ✗ Individuals requiring skilled nursing services beyond our service authorization
  • ✗ Those requiring acute medical monitoring or hospital-level care
  • ✗ Individuals whose behavioral or safety needs exceed our current capabilities
  • ✗ Residents requiring services not authorized under our license
Even if we're not the right fit, we'll do our best to point you toward appropriate resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Families

What is the difference between assisted living and a nursing home? +
Assisted living provides help with daily activities (bathing, meals, medication reminders) in a residential setting, while nursing homes offer higher levels of medical and skilled nursing care. Sygma House is an assisted living home — residents who require skilled nursing or hospital-level care may need a different setting.
Can families visit anytime? +
Yes — families are welcome. We encourage ongoing family involvement in care. We ask that visits respect residents' rest times, but there are no rigid restrictions on family contact.
How does the Medicaid waiver admission process work? +
For Elderly Waiver (EW), the individual typically must be enrolled in Medical Assistance (MA), receive a MnCHOICES assessment that confirms nursing-home level of care need, and work with a county case manager or lead agency. Once authorized, we can receive waiver-funded residents. The timeline depends on county processes and eligibility determination.
What happens if my loved one's care needs increase over time? +
Service plans are reviewed and updated regularly. If care needs increase beyond what Sygma House can safely provide, we will work with the family and care team to make an appropriate transition plan — we will never abandon a resident without proper planning and notice.
Is Sygma House licensed by the state of Minnesota? +
Sygma House operates as a Minnesota assisted living facility. [Licensing details and license number to be confirmed and posted here once issued.] Minnesota assisted living facilities are licensed by the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) under Chapter 144G.
What should I bring when my loved one moves in? +
We recommend bringing personal clothing and shoes, toiletry items, favorite photos or comfort items, and any personal bedding/blankets the resident prefers. Bring all medications and a current medication list. Electronics like phones or tablets are welcome. We'll provide a move-in guide with a complete list.
How do I file a complaint or concern? +
We always encourage families and residents to bring concerns to us directly first. You may also contact the Minnesota Department of Health (MDH) Office of Health Facility Complaints. The Long-Term Care Ombudsman program is available as an independent advocate for residents.

⚖️ Resident Rights

Residents of Minnesota assisted living facilities have legally protected rights under Minnesota Statute Section 144G.91. These include but are not limited to:

  • ✓ The right to be treated with dignity and respect
  • ✓ The right to privacy and confidentiality
  • ✓ The right to participate in care planning decisions
  • ✓ The right to receive visitors and communicate freely
  • ✓ The right to file complaints without fear of retaliation
  • ✓ The right to be free from abuse, neglect, and exploitation
  • ✓ The right to manage personal finances (or have a representative)
